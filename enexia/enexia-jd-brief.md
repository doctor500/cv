## 2. Company Snapshot

- **Industry & Product:** Renewable energy aggregator and technology platform. Enexia aggregates energy from diverse renewable assets (solar, wind, batteries), forecasts generation and pricing, manages imbalance settlements, and trades electricity in Japan's JEPX power spot market. The name combines ENERGY + NEXT + AXIS (軸/axis) — meaning "becoming the next axis of the energy industry." `[jd-senior-platform-engineer.txt, lines 8-138]` `[enexia-notes.txt, line 1]`
- **Parent company:** Part of Sumitomo Corporation (one of Japan's largest conglomerates). `[titan-gt-briefing.txt, line 9]`
- **Company size/stage:** Startup scale — approximately 6 data scientists, 3 engineers (+ vendors like SCSK, Insight), total 10-20 contributors. `[enexia-notes.txt, line 2]`
- **Strategic goals:** Become #1 energy aggregator in Japan within 2-3 years; expand into Europe and Southeast Asia long-term. `[titan-gt-briefing.txt, lines 15-16]` `[enexia-notes.txt, line 3]`
- **Engineering culture signals:** Flat organization with strong technical team composed of core members from top universities. `[titan-gt-gt-briefing.txt, line 14]` Cross-functional collaboration between Data Science and Engineering is critical. DS code often not production-ready, so engineers who can bridge the gap are highly valued. `[enexia-notes.txt, line 2]` "Role for builders, innovators, and collaborators." `[jd-senior-platform-engineer.txt, lines 224-225]`
